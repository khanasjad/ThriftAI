# Veritas Score™ - Complete Parameter Breakdown

## Overview
The Veritas Score is a **0-100 universal product scoring system** that predicts purchase likelihood and product quality. It analyzes products across **9 major categories** with **116+ enriched parameters**.

## Score Calculation Formula

```
Veritas Score = (
  (priceValue × 0.20) +
  (trustScore × 0.16) +
  (specsQuality × 0.15) +
  (socialProof × 0.13) +
  (qualityScore × 0.11) +
  (userExperience × 0.10) +
  (relevance × 0.07) +
  (urgency × 0.04) +
  (emotional × 0.04)
)
```

**Final Score Range**: 0-100

- Trust Score < 30
