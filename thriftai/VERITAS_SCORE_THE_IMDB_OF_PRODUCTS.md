# VERITAS SCORE™
## The IMDB of Products

> **Just like IMDB revolutionized movie ratings, Veritas Score™ is transforming how we evaluate products in the secondhand marketplace.**

---

## 🎯 Vision Statement

**IMDB** made it simple: one score tells you if a movie is worth watching.
**Veritas Score™** does the same for products: one score tells you if a product is worth buying.

**The Problem:**
- Buying secondhand products is risky and uncertain
- No standardized quality measurement exists
- Buyers rely on limited seller descriptions and photos
- Product quality varies wildly across platforms

**The Solution:**
- A universal, objective quality score (0-100)
- Backed by 121 verified parameters
- Transparent, data-driven methodology
- Works like a credit score for products

```
Veritas Score = Weighted sum of 8 category scores (0-100)
```

## 🏆 The 8 Categories

### 1. Product Quality (25% Weight) ⭐
**"The Core Score" - Like IMDB's Production Quality**

What it measures:
- Physical condition and wear level
- Visual defects and damage assessment
- Functional completeness
- Material quality and authenticity
- Manufacturing standards

**Why it matters:** Just like you wouldn't watch a poorly produced movie, you shouldn't buy a low-quality product.

**Sample Parameters:**
- Product Condition Score (10% weight)
- Visual Defects via AI Analysis (8% weight)
- Functional Completeness (9% weight)
- Wear and Tear Level (7% weight)
- Missing Components Check (6% weight)
- Material Quality Assessment (5% weight)

**Scoring Example:**
```
New iPhone 12: 95/100 (Excellent condition)
Used iPhone 12 with scratches: 72/100 (Good condition)
Broken iPhone 12: 25/100 (Poor condition)
```

**"The Director Score" - Like IMDB's Director Reputation**

What it measures:
- Seller rating history
- Response time and communication
- Transaction success rate
- Return policy quality
- Dispute resolution record

**Why it matters:** A great product from a bad seller is still a bad deal. Just like a talented director elevates a movie, a trustworthy seller enhances product value.

## 📱 Score Serial Number (SSN)

**Every Veritas Score has a unique identifier**

### Format
```
VS-{CATEGORY}-{SCORE}-{CONFIDENCE}-{DATE}

Example: VS-ELE-087-82-20251003
```

### Components
- **VS:** Veritas Score prefix
- **ELE:** Product category code (ELECTRONICS)
- **087:** Overall score (87/100)
- **82:** Confidence level (82%)
- **20251003:** Calculation date (Oct 3, 2025)

### Why SSN Matters

**Traceability:**
```
✓ Track score changes over time
✓ Verify score authenticity
✓ Prevent score manipulation
✓ Historical comparison
✓ Audit trail for disputes
```

**Like VIN for cars:**
- Every score is unique
- Permanent record
- Blockchain-ready format
- Searchable and verifiable
